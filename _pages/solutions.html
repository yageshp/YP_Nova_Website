Visitor Management System

A digital record of who is entering your premises, who they are meeting and when they leave.

Business problem

Paper registers at the gate are slow to fill in, hard to read later and almost impossible to search or report on when someone asks who visited a site.

Proposed solution

A web-based visitor system where entries are recorded digitally, hosts are identified, and visit history is searchable by date, person or location.

What we have delivered

  • A Visitor Management System deployed on a VPS for a facility management and MEP operations client.

Business benefits

  • Legible, searchable visitor records
  • Clear accountability at entry points
  • Less time spent on manual registers

Core modules · confirmed per project

  • Visitor registration
  • Host and purpose-of-visit capture
  • Check-in and check-out log
  • Visit history and search
  • Reports

Workflow

  1. 01Visitor arrives and details are recorded
  2. 02Host and purpose are confirmed
  3. 03Entry is logged with time
  4. 04Exit is recorded
  5. 05Records are available for review and reporting

User roles

  • Security / reception staff
  • Hosts and employees
  • Site administrators
  • Management

Relevant industries

  • Facility Management
  • Security Services
  • Construction & MEP
  • Manufacturing

Optional future enhancements

Not part of the base scope — added only if you need them.

  • Pre-registration by hosts
  • Visitor passes or badges
  • Host notifications

Built by YP Nova in a client project.
