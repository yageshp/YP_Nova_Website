Facility Management

Multi-site visitor management

Typical scenario

A facilities operator wants one visitor record across several buildings instead of a paper register at each gate.

How we would approach it

A web-based visitor system with site-wise entry screens, host lookup and a central visit history that supervisors can search and export.

Intended value

Consistent visitor records across sites and faster answers when an entry needs to be checked.

  • Visitor entry
  • Site master
  • Visit history
  • Reports

Manufacturing

Plant MIS and KPI dashboard

Typical scenario

A manufacturer’s leadership reviews performance from spreadsheets prepared separately by production, stores and purchase.

How we would approach it

An MIS that pulls the departmental sheets together and presents agreed KPIs on dashboards, with Power BI for deeper analysis.

Intended value

One set of numbers for reviews, and less time spent preparing them.

  • Data connections
  • Department reports
  • KPI dashboard
  • Power BI

Construction & MEP

Digital work permit workflow

Typical scenario

A contractor needs to see, at any moment, which jobs on site are authorised and who approved them.

How we would approach it

Permit requests raised by contractors, reviewed by site engineers and safety officers in sequence, with an active-permit board for the site office.

Intended value

A documented approval trail and a live view of authorised work.

  • Permit request
  • Approval levels
  • Active permit board
  • Permit register

Retail

Store issue and complaint tracking

Typical scenario

A retail chain receives maintenance and customer issues from many outlets by phone and message.

How we would approach it

A single complaint log where each outlet raises issues by category, the central team assigns them, and status is visible to everyone involved.

Intended value

Clear ownership of every issue and visibility of repeat problems by outlet.

  • Issue logging
  • Assignment
  • Status tracking
  • Outlet reports

Real Estate

Property operations portal

Typical scenario

A property manager wants occupant requests, visitor entry and common-area maintenance handled in one system.

How we would approach it

A portal combining an occupant help-desk, gate visitor entry and maintenance tracking, with summary reports for owners.

Intended value

Organised handling of occupant requests and a documented record of site activity.

  • Occupant help-desk

Healthcare

Administrative workflow automation

Typical scenario

A healthcare provider’s back-office team re-types information from forms and emails into several systems.

How we would approach it

Document processing to extract form data for staff to verify, with approval steps and automatic updates to the relevant records.

Intended value

Less repetitive data entry, with people reviewing and approving rather than typing.

  • Document intake
  • AI-assisted extraction
  • Review and approval
  • Record updates
