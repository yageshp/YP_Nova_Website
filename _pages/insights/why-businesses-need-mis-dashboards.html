Data & Reporting

How MIS Software Improves Business Operations

If preparing the monthly report takes days, the report is already out of date. An MIS changes reporting from an event into something that is simply available.

4 min read · By the YP Nova team

Reporting as a recurring cost

In many organisations, capable people spend a meaningful part of each month collecting sheets, merging them and checking totals. The work is repetitive, and the result is a snapshot that starts ageing the moment it is sent.

What an MIS changes

A Management Information System connects to the places where data is already recorded and organises it into consistent reports and dashboards. The figures are calculated the same way every time, and managers can look at them when they need to rather than when the report arrives.

Start from the data you have

An MIS does not require replacing how teams work. In our own project experience, an MIS reads operational data from Google Sheets through the Google Sheets API, so site teams keep the entry method they know while management gets dashboards and meter-reading reports. The system is described in our MIS solution and operations case study.

Choosing what to show

A useful dashboard answers a small number of agreed questions. Begin with the figures you already review in meetings, define each one precisely, and add more only when they are needed for a decision.
