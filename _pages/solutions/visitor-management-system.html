Overview

A visitor management system (VMS) records who enters your premises, whom they are visiting, why, and when they arrive and leave — in a digital register that can be searched and reported on. YP Nova has delivered a VMS deployed on a VPS for a facility management and MEP operations client.

The problem

Paper registers at the gate are slow to fill in, hard to read later and almost impossible to search or report on when someone asks who visited a site.

The solution

A web-based visitor system where entries are recorded digitally, hosts are identified, and visit history is searchable by date, person or location.

What we have delivered

  • A Visitor Management System deployed on a VPS for a facility management and MEP operations client.

Core modules

  • Visitor registration
  • Host and purpose-of-visit capture
  • Check-in and check-out log
  • Visit history and search
  • Reports

Frequently asked questions

What is a visitor management system?

It is software that replaces the paper visitor register: entries are captured digitally with visitor, host, purpose and time, and the history can be searched and reported on.

Can it work across multiple sites?

Yes. Entries are recorded per site, and supervisors or management can view and search history across all sites according to their role.

Does the visitor system need special hardware?

No. It runs in a web browser on a desktop, tablet or phone at the gate. Optional additions such as badge printing are scoped per project.

Where is the data hosted?

Our delivered VMS runs on a VPS. Hosting is agreed with you before deployment, including access and retention.
