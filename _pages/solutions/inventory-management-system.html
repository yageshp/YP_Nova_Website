Inventory Management System

Stock, issues and receipts tracked by item and location so you know what you have and where it is.

Custom development Designed and built to your requirements.
Discuss this solution

Overview

A custom inventory management system records every stock movement — receipts, issues and transfers — so balances by item and location are always current. It is offered as custom development and is commonly the first module of a custom ERP.

The problem

Stock is tracked in spreadsheets that fall out of date, leading to shortages on site, duplicate purchases and time lost counting.

The solution

A custom inventory application with item masters, stock movements and location-wise balances, shaped to your stores and issue process.

Core modules

  • Item master
  • Receipts and issues
  • Location-wise stock
  • Minimum-level alerts
  • Stock reports

The final module list is confirmed for each project.

User roles

  • Storekeepers
  • Requesters / site teams
  • Purchase team
  • Management

Typical workflow

  1. 01Material is received and recorded
  2. 02Stock is issued against a request
  3. 03Balances update automatically
  4. 04Low stock is flagged
  5. 05Reports support purchasing decisions

Business benefits

  • Current stock position at any time
  • Traceable issues and receipts
  • Better-informed purchasing

Integration possibilities

  • Procurement module (purchase orders and receipts)
  • Accounting software via export or API
  • Barcode / QR labels (optional)
  • Power BI consumption analysis

Optional enhancements

  • Barcode or QR labels
  • Link to purchase orders
  • Consumption analysis

Industry use cases

  • Manufacturing Raw material and spares stores with issues against production or maintenance requests.
  • Construction & MEP Site stores with material issued against work fronts.
  • Retail Warehouse-to-outlet transfers and outlet stock levels.
  • Logistics Warehouse stock linked to inbound and outbound consignments.

Frequently asked questions

What does an inventory management system track?

Item details, stock received, issued and transferred, current balances by location, and items below their minimum level.

Can it handle multiple stores or warehouses?

Yes. Stock is held per location, with transfers between locations recorded.

Can it link to purchasing?

Yes. Low stock can raise purchase requisitions and goods are received against purchase orders when combined with the procurement module.
