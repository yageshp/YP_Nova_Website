1 · Project overview

One environment for daily operations

YP Nova designed and delivered a set of connected web applications for a facility management and MEP operations client: a Management Information System, a Visitor Management System, a Work Permit Management System and a Complaint Management System. Together they give site teams, security, contractors, the help-desk and management one environment for records that were previously kept on paper and in separate spreadsheets.

2 · Business context

Facility and MEP operations produce a steady flow of operational records every day: visitors arriving at the gate, contractors needing authorisation for work, occupants reporting faults, and utility meter readings that management needs to review. Each of these was handled separately, which made it slow to answer simple questions about what happened on a given day.

3 · Operational challenges

  • Visitor entries kept in paper registers that were hard to search later
  • Permit requests and approvals moving on paper, with no easy view of what was approved or active
  • Complaints received by phone and message, without a single register or clear ownership
  • Meter readings recorded in spreadsheets and consolidated by hand for management reports
  • Different people needing different levels of access to the same information

These technologies were used across the project. Module-specific facts are listed only where confirmed — for example, the MIS reads data through the Google Sheets API, and the VMS is deployed on a VPS. Not every technology applies to every module.

10 · Integration overview

  • Google Sheets → MIS

    The MIS reads operational data, including meter readings, from the client’s Google Sheets through the Google Sheets API.

  • VMS + Work Permits

    The Work Permit System is deployed within the same VPS-hosted environment as the Visitor Management System.

  • Complaints ↔ MIS / VMS

    Complaint management is integrated into the MIS / VMS environment rather than running as a separate tool.

  • Role-based access

    Users see the information and actions their role allows, in both the MIS and complaint management.

11 · Delivery approach

How we approach every project

  1. 01

    Requirement understanding

    We learn how the work is done today — the people involved, the records kept and where time or information is lost — before proposing anything.

  2. 02

    Solution planning

    Modules, user roles, data structure, integrations and phases are agreed and written down, so scope and priorities are clear to everyone.

  3. 03

    Design and development

    Screens are designed around the real workflow and built in stages. You review working software regularly rather than waiting for the end.

  4. 04

    Testing

    Each module is tested against real scenarios and edge cases with your team before it is released for daily use.

  5. 05

    Deployment

    The application is deployed to the agreed hosting — for example a VPS — with users, roles and access configured and initial training given.

  6. 06

    Support and improvements

    After go-live we fix issues, keep the system maintained and add improvements that real use reveals, as agreed with you.

12 · Business value

What the system changes

  • Structured operational records instead of paper registers and scattered files
  • Improved visibility of operational information for management
  • Digitalised request and approval workflows for site work
  • Centralised access to selected operational information, by role
  • More organised complaint tracking, with ownership and status for each complaint

Value is described qualitatively: no measured performance figures have been verified and approved for publication.
