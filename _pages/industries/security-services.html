Software for Security Services

Security agencies manage a large, distributed workforce and are accountable for what happens at every gate. We build systems for deployment, attendance, visitor control and client reporting.

YP Nova is the technology brand of YP Security Services Pvt. Ltd., so we understand guarding operations first-hand.

Industry challenges

  • Guards deployed across many client sites and shifts
  • Clients expecting proof of deployment and incident handling
  • High administrative load for attendance, rosters and billing support

Day-to-day operational problems

  • Attendance collected by phone and consolidated at month-end
  • Visitor and vehicle entries written in registers
  • Incidents reported informally with no structured follow-up

Relevant solutions

What we would build

The solutions most often relevant to this industry. Each is configured to your sites, teams and reports.

HR & Workforce Management Employee records, deployment, attendance and leave managed in one system for distributed teams.
Visitor Management System A digital record of who is entering your premises, who they are meeting and when they leave.
Complaint Management System Complaints and service requests logged in one place, assigned to someone and tracked until they are closed.
Business Intelligence Dashboards KPI dashboards built on your existing data using custom dashboards or Power BI.

Typical workflow

How it works in practice

  1. 01Guards are assigned to sites and shifts
  2. 02Attendance is recorded per site each day
  3. 03Visitors are logged at the gate in the visitor system
  4. 04Incidents are recorded and assigned for follow-up
  5. 05Clients and management receive consistent reports

Potential modules

  • Guard records and deployment
  • Attendance
  • Visitor and gate entry
  • Incident log
  • Client-wise reports

The final module list is agreed with you during discovery.

Benefits you can expect

  • A reliable deployment and attendance record
  • Professional, consistent reporting to clients
  • Less month-end compilation effort
