What is agentic AI?

Agentic AI describes AI systems that do more than answer a single question. An agentic system is given a goal, plans the steps needed, uses tools — such as searching documents, reading a database or calling an API — checks the result, and continues until the task is done or it needs a person to decide.

For a business, the useful question is not whether a system is “agentic” but which multi-step tasks it can reliably carry out, what it is allowed to change, and where a human must approve.

What are AI agents?

An AI agent is a software component built around a large language model that can take actions through defined tools. A support-triage agent might read an incoming request, look up the customer’s history, classify the issue and draft a reply for a staff member to approve. Each tool the agent can use is explicitly granted and logged.

Agentic AI vs traditional automation
AspectTraditional workflow automationAgentic AI
Works best withStructured data and fixed rulesUnstructured text, documents and judgement-like steps
BehaviourFollows the same path every timeChooses steps and tools to reach a goal
PredictabilityFully predictableNeeds limits, checks and review points
Typical exampleSend approval request when a form is submittedRead a supplier email, extract the order details and prepare a draft purchase request

Most real solutions combine both: rule-based business process automation for the predictable steps, and AI only where language or documents are involved.

AI agent architecture and AI integration services

A production AI agent is a small system rather than a single prompt. We design four parts: the model, chosen for the task and data-handling needs; the tools the agent may use, each a narrow, audited function such as “search documents” or “create draft record”; the context it is given, retrieved from your approved sources; and the controls — permissions, limits, logging and approval steps.

Agents connect to your environment through APIs — your custom business application, databases, Google Sheets or other systems that offer access.

Document and knowledge-base automation

Knowledge-base assistants use retrieval: your documents are indexed, the most relevant passages are found for each question, and the model answers from those passages only. Answers can show their source so staff can check them, and access can follow the same roles as your business application.

AI assistants for business operations

An assistant inside an existing application is often the most practical starting point: a “summarise this record”, “draft a reply” or “explain this report” action placed where staff already work, rather than a separate chatbot. See how this applies in AI in day-to-day business operations.

Human approval and oversight workflows

  • Agents prepare drafts and suggestions; a named person approves anything that changes records, money or customer communication.
  • Every tool call and decision is logged for review.
  • Confidence thresholds route uncertain cases to people rather than guessing.
  • Agents can be paused or limited per task without changing the rest of the system.

Enterprise AI solutions: security and data considerations

Before building, we agree which data the AI may see, where it is processed, how long it is kept and who can use each feature. Sensitive fields can be excluded or masked, access follows user roles, and the choice of AI model provider is made with your data-handling requirements in mind. AI output is treated as a draft that can be wrong, and the design reflects that.

Frequently asked questions

What is agentic AI development?

It is the design and development of AI systems that can carry out multi-step tasks — planning steps, using approved tools such as document search or APIs, and checking results — within limits and approval points you define.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software built around a language model that can take actions through specific, permitted tools. For example, it can read a request, look up related records and prepare a draft response for a staff member to approve.

What business tasks can AI agents automate?

Good candidates involve reading and organising information: triaging requests, extracting data from documents, answering questions from internal knowledge, summarising reports and preparing drafts. Tasks with legal, financial or safety consequences should keep a human approval step.

Can AI agents connect to our existing business software?

Yes, where the software offers an API or another reliable access method. The agent is given narrow tools — for example “create a draft ticket” — rather than broad access, and every action is logged.

How are AI agents monitored and controlled?

Through permissions on each tool, approval steps for consequential actions, logs of every decision and action, confidence thresholds that send uncertain cases to people, and the ability to pause an agent at any time.

Do you offer generative AI development?

Yes, as custom development: assistants that answer from your own documents, drafting and summarising features inside business applications, and document processing — each with review steps and access controls agreed in advance.

Has YP Nova deployed AI agents for clients?

Not yet as a published case study. Our delivered work is in business applications, MIS dashboards and workflow systems. AI agents and assistants are offered as custom development, and we recommend starting with a tested prototype on one workflow.
