1. Who we are

YP Nova is the technology brand of YP Security Services Pvt. Ltd. (“YP Nova”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through our website, ypnova.com. For the purposes of applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (as its provisions apply), YP Security Services Pvt. Ltd. decides why and how this personal data is processed.

This policy covers the website only. Personal data handled during a software project is covered by the agreement for that project.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us through the enquiry form:

  • full name and work email address;
  • company name, phone number and industry (optional);
  • the service you need, an approximate budget (optional) and your project description;
  • your preferred contact method and your confirmation that we may contact you;
  • the address of the page from which the form was sent.

Information you give us directly when you call, email or message us on WhatsApp — for example your name, number and the content of your message.

Technical information: like most websites, the servers of our hosting provider record standard technical information about requests to the website (such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request) to deliver and secure the site.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data such as financial account details, health information or government identification numbers through this website. Please do not include them in your enquiry.

3. How we use your information

  • to read and respond to your enquiry using the contact method you prefer;
  • to discuss your requirements and prepare a proposal or estimate you have asked for;
  • to keep a record of our communication with you;
  • to keep the website working, secure and protected against misuse and spam.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. How the enquiry form works

When you send the enquiry form, your details are transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a Google Apps Script service operated by us. It adds your enquiry to a Google Sheet in our Google account and sends a notification email to info@ypnova.com. Your enquiry is therefore stored and processed using Google services, under Google’s terms and privacy policy, and may be processed on servers outside India.

If online sending is unavailable, the form offers to open your own email application with your details pre-filled; nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

The form includes basic automated spam protection. A submission that appears to be automated is not sent.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies, and at the date of this policy it does not use analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are served from our own website, not from a third-party font service.

The website has been prepared so that Google Analytics can be switched on in future to measure visits and enquiries. We will update this policy before analytics is enabled.

The interactive product demonstrations on the website use fictional sample data; anything you enter in them stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

6. Third-party services

  • Google (Google Workspace / Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) — receiving and storing enquiries and notification emails.
  • Our web hosting provider (MilesWeb) — hosting the website and its standard server logs.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — only if you choose to contact us by WhatsApp; the WhatsApp link opens their service.
  • Google Maps — only if you open our Google Maps link; no map is embedded in the website.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. We share personal data with others only where required by law.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above — for example while we are discussing your requirements and for a reasonable period afterwards to keep a record of that communication — or for longer where the law requires it. We delete or anonymise enquiry records that are no longer needed.

8. How we protect information

The website is served over HTTPS, and enquiry records are kept in a Google account to which only authorised people at YP Nova have access. We take reasonable care to protect personal data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it when it is no longer needed;
  • stop contacting you — you can withdraw your consent at any time;
  • address any grievance about how your data has been handled.

Send your request to info@ypnova.com or call +91 82914 19044. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before the withdrawal.

10. Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. The current version and its “last updated” date are always shown on this page.

12. Contact for privacy questions
