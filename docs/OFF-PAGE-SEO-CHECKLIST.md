# Local & off-page SEO checklist

The website is now technically ready. Rankings for competitive searches also depend on signals from
outside the site, built gradually and genuinely. **Never buy links, create fake listings or post fake
reviews.** Google penalises them, and they damage trust with customers.

## 1. Google Business Profile — highest priority for local searches
- [ ] Create or claim the profile at https://business.google.com.
- [ ] **Name:** `YP Nova`, exactly as on the website (no extra keywords in the name).
- [ ] **Primary category:** *Software company*. **Additional:** *Computer consultant* /
      *Website designer* only if genuinely offered. Choose from Google's list.
- [ ] **Phone:** `+91 82914 19044` · **Website:** `https://ypnova.com`
- [ ] **Location:** if customers do not visit the office, set it as a *service-area business* and
      hide the address. Add service area: India (international clients are served
      remotely and mentioned in the description).
- [ ] **Description** (750 characters max; plain and factual), for example: "YP Nova is the technology brand of YP
      Security Services Pvt. Ltd. We build custom business software, ERP modules, MIS and Power BI
      dashboards, business process automation and AI-assisted tools — including visitor management,
      work permit and complaint management systems."
- [ ] **Services:** add each service with a link to its page (e.g. Custom ERP Development →
      https://ypnova.com/custom-erp-development).
- [ ] Add the logo, a cover image and genuine photos (team, office, work in progress; no stock photos).
- [ ] Check the map pin matches https://maps.app.goo.gl/Y3U94Lmn99JVwu2v9.
- [ ] Post an update monthly (a new article, a delivered project, an announcement).

## 2. Genuine reviews
- [ ] Ask satisfied clients directly for a Google review, sending the profile's review link.
- [ ] Reply to every review politely, including critical ones.
- [ ] Do not offer incentives for reviews and do not review your own business.
- [ ] Do not add review stars to the website's structured data. Google ignores self-served review
      markup for organisations.

## 3. Consistent business listings (NAP: Name, Address/Area, Phone)
Use exactly the same name, phone, website and description everywhere:
- [ ] Bing Places for Business (can import from Google Business Profile)
- [ ] LinkedIn Company Page (then add its URL to `src/config/company.js → social`)
- [ ] Justdial · Sulekha · IndiaMART (only categories you genuinely serve)
- [ ] Clutch.co and GoodFirms (B2B software directories; their reviews are verified with real clients)
- [ ] Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps)
- [ ] Facebook / Instagram business pages, only if you will maintain them

## 4. Relevant, earned links and mentions
- [ ] **Client case-study link:** with Reliable Group's permission, ask for a short mention or link on
      their site, and publish their name on your case study.
- [ ] **Parent company:** link from YP Security Services' website (if it has one) to ypnova.com, and
      describe YP Nova as its technology brand.
- [ ] **Industry associations and chambers:** membership listings in relevant facility-management,
      construction/MEP, security-services or IT associations you genuinely join, and local chambers of
      commerce in Mumbai / Navi Mumbai.
- [ ] **Technology partners:** if you become a listed partner of a platform you use, request a partner
      directory listing.
- [ ] **Guest contributions:** practical articles for facility-management, construction or SME business
      publications, e.g. "How to digitise work permits on a construction site".
- [ ] **Talks and events:** local business, startup or industry events often publish speaker pages
      with links.

## 5. Content that attracts links
- [ ] Publish one useful article a month in the Insights section (keep to the topic map in
      SEO-KEYWORD-MAP.md).
- [ ] Turn each new delivered project into a case study, with the client's permission.
- [ ] Share each article on LinkedIn from the company page and from personal profiles.

## 6. Avoid
Paid link packages · private blog networks · directory blasts and automated submissions · comment or
forum spam · duplicate city pages · fake addresses or virtual offices presented as real offices ·
keyword-stuffed business names.
